Countdown World Cup 2026 Rainmeter Skin

Taken from the project "Countdown in Lua" (https://visualskins.com/skin/countdown-lua) - by 66VI (https://www.deviantart.com/66vi).

To change the logo replace the logo.png with any image you want.

To change the date to count down to, open the countdown.ini file and change the numbers in the [Variables] section, they are called toYear, toMonth, etc.  You can also change the text to be displayed when the date is reached by changing the ReleaseText variable.

A countdown timer has been added for the kickoff of the opening match of the 2026 FIFA World Cup between Mexico and South Africa on June 11. The local time must be adjusted in the countdown.ini file (toHour variable).

The official logo of the 2026 FIFA World Cup was also added, as well as its "We Are 26" typeface.

Enjoy!
